## 1. 钩子的定位与对比机制

"The biggest difference between winning and losing on social media is the hook. This is the 80/20 of video."

"The viewer starts the video believing one thing, and then we introduce an alternative. They believe A, we show them B, and the distance between A and B is contrast."

"The contrast word is comparing the new outcome, the objective we just accomplished versus the base state. It is meant to intensify the curiosity going from A to B."

## 2. 三拍公式（Context Lean → Scroll-Stop → Contrarian Snapback）

"The contrarian snapback is a sentence that goes in the opposite direction of the initial lean. It's still on topic but it snaps the viewer back on a different path."

示例（Sphere 视频，分析页标注约 8M 播放）：
- Lean: "The tech in the Vegas sphere is insane — biggest screen ever built, 20 times bigger than an IMAX."
- Stop: "But get this — the screen is actually the least impressive part of the whole thing."
- Snapback: "Because the most impressive part is the audio. This is going to blow your mind."

## 3. 六槽语法（Subject / Action / Objective / Contrast + Proof + Time）

"All you need to know are six power words. If you can understand these six words and why they work so well to stop the scroll, you can essentially become a master of content psychology overnight."

"If a hook describes only the after-state without an implied 'loss-state-it-flips,' it has objective without contrast and underperforms."

槽位坍缩（分析页归纳）："0 to 100K" 同时是 objective 与 contrast；"before you build it" 同时是 contrast 与 time。

## 4. 四错诊断（Delay / Confusion / Irrelevance / Disinterest）

"A curiosity loop is when the viewer sees something, asks a hypothetical question in their mind, gets some additional context to answer it, but that spurs a new question, more context, new question, more context."

改写方向（分析页归纳）：代词从「我」换成「你」；把"有也不错"改成"必须有"；对比要么明说 A 与 B，要么依赖受众共享的基线（团队判断：基线不确定时明说）。

## 5. 六原型（按可用的视觉挑，而非按选题挑）

原型清单（分析页归纳）：Fortune Teller / Experimenter / Teacher / Magician / Investigator / Contrarian。
其中 Magician 只作修饰层："Without a contrast layer it is empty spectacle."

"Every single video idea could be manipulated to use any of the six hook formats."

## 6. 四通道对齐（视觉 / 口播 / 屏幕文字 / 音频）

"Every hook is four aligned signals"（原句由四通道展开）。

"The difference between 500 and 500,000 views is unlocking max alignment between those four things."

"The most important part of getting the hook to actually perform well is the visual. It's not what you say. It's the visual."

"Our eyes process 10 to 100 times more information per second than our ears — speed of light versus speed of sound."

## 7. 反模式（与分析页一致）

"If you just do this game but then you don't actually have anything at the end of the rainbow, people would just churn and you're a fraud."

其他反模式：用空悬念拖时间；术语堆叠；抽象口播配具象画面导致对不上；把最强视觉留到钩子之后；视觉弱仍硬做这条视频。

## 8. 生产参数与训练法（分析页转述）

- 首个价值点：短视频约 4 秒内，长视频约 1–2 分钟。
- 屏幕文字：3–5 个词，写品类或承诺，不写功能名。
- 钩子句：短句，每句约 12 词以内。
- 训练法（copy work）：拆同行爆款的第一句 → 标注六个槽位 → 删掉题材词 → 用自己的题材重新填槽。
- AI 用法：只做「清晰化改写」（六年级阅读水平、同义不改意），不从空白生成钩子。

## 9. 与实证证据的关系（本文件不提供证据）
